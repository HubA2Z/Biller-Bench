# Question
In the hospital, our NP and physician both see the same patient on the same day. Who bills the visit, and is there a modifier?

# Answer
**Short answer:** in a **facility setting** (hospital or SNF), Medicare lets a physician and NPP in the same group bill one **split (or shared) visit** under whoever performed the **substantive portion**, with modifier **FS**.

## The substantive portion
Under current Medicare rules, the billing practitioner must perform:
- **more than half of the total time** spent by both, **or**
- a **substantive part of the medical decision making**.

Check CMS's current Physician Fee Schedule rule each year, as this definition has changed in recent years.

## Rules
1. Only in facility settings; office visits use incident-to rules instead.
2. Add modifier **FS** to the claim.
3. Both practitioners should document their parts, and whose work was substantive should be clear.

## Note
Commercial plans may not recognize split or shared billing. Check each plan's policy.
