# Question
We billed Original Medicare using the patient's Medicare card and got a denial saying to bill another payer (CO-109). The patient insists they have Medicare.

# Answer
**Short answer:** most likely the patient is enrolled in a **Medicare Advantage (Part C) plan**. They still have their Medicare card, but the Medicare Advantage plan pays their claims, not Original Medicare.

## How to check
1. Run a Medicare eligibility check (your MAC's portal or a HETS-based eligibility tool). It shows Medicare Advantage enrollment and the plan.
2. Ask the patient for **every** insurance card they have; they often carry both.

## How to fix it
1. Bill the Medicare Advantage plan, using its rules for authorizations and network status.
2. Watch the plan's filing deadline; it's often shorter than Medicare's.

## Prevent it
- Check eligibility at **every** visit; patients change plans each year, especially in January.
