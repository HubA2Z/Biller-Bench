# Question
Our NP sees follow-up patients in the office. Can we bill these under the physician to get the full rate instead of 85%?

# Answer
**Short answer:** only if **all incident-to rules** are met. Otherwise the NP bills under their own NPI, and Medicare pays 85% of the physician fee schedule.

## Medicare incident-to requirements (office setting)
1. The **physician** saw the patient first and started the plan of care for that problem.
2. The NP's visit **follows that plan of care** (no new problems, and no change to the plan).
3. A physician of the group gives **direct supervision**, meaning they're available in the office suite (check CMS's current direct supervision rules).
4. The NP is an employee or contractor of the practice.

## When it can't be incident-to
- New patients, or new problems for established patients.
- Hospital and facility settings.
- When no supervising physician was in the office suite.

## Note
Commercial plans set their own rules, and many don't recognize incident-to at all.
