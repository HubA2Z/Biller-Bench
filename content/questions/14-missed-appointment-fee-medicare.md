# Question
We want to start charging a fee when patients miss appointments without notice. Are we allowed to charge Medicare patients?

# Answer
**Short answer:** yes. Medicare allows providers to charge **missed appointment fees**, as long as the **same policy applies to all patients**, not only Medicare patients.

## Good practice
1. Put the policy in writing and give it to patients in advance (for example, in the new patient paperwork).
2. Apply it the same way to every patient.
3. Bill the fee to the **patient**, not Medicare. A missed appointment isn't a Medicare-covered service.

## Medicaid and other plans
Medicaid rules differ: many states don't allow charging Medicaid patients for missed appointments. Check your state Medicaid rules and your commercial contracts.
