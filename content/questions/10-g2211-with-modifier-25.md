# Question
We bill G2211 with ongoing-care office visits. When the visit also has a procedure and modifier 25, Medicare denies G2211. Is that right?

# Answer
**Short answer:** usually, yes. Medicare doesn't pay G2211 when the office visit is billed with **modifier 25**, with exceptions starting in 2025: G2211 **is** payable when the modifier 25 visit is billed on the same day as an **annual wellness visit, a vaccine administration, or a Medicare Part B preventive service**.

## What G2211 is for
G2211 is an add-on for the complexity of office and outpatient visits when the practitioner is the continuing focal point for all needed care, or gives ongoing care for a single serious or complex condition.

## How to handle denials
1. If the modifier 25 visit was with a procedure (for example, an injection), the G2211 denial is correct.
2. If it was with an AWV, vaccine administration or Part B preventive service, the G2211 should pay; ask for a reopening if it didn't.
3. Make sure the documentation shows the ongoing relationship.

## Note
Commercial and Medicare Advantage plans set their own G2211 rules. Check each plan's policy, and check CMS's current fee schedule rule each January.
