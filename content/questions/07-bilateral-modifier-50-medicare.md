# Question
We injected both knees on the same day. Should we bill 20610 twice with RT and LT, or once with modifier 50? We've seen both get denied.

# Answer
**Short answer:** for Medicare, check the code's **bilateral surgery indicator** in the Medicare Physician Fee Schedule. When the indicator is **1**, bill **one line, modifier 50, 1 unit**. Medicare pays 150% of the fee schedule amount for the code.

## The indicators
- **0**: bilateral rules don't apply; modifier 50 isn't allowed.
- **1**: bilateral payment rules apply; use modifier 50 on one line.
- **2**: the code's value already covers both sides; don't use 50.
- **3**: each side is paid at 100%; RT and LT on separate lines are typical.

## Commercial payers
Many commercial payers prefer **two lines with RT and LT**, or 50 with **2 units**. Always check the payer's bilateral billing policy, because the format that works for Medicare can be denied elsewhere.
