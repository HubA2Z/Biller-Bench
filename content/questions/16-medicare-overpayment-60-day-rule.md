# Question
During an internal review we found visits that were billed at a higher level than documented and paid by Medicare. What's the deadline to pay it back?

# Answer
**Short answer:** a Medicare overpayment must be **reported and returned within 60 days after it's identified** (or by the related cost report's due date, if later). Keeping it longer can create liability under the False Claims Act.

While you do a timely, good-faith investigation to find related overpayments with the same cause, the 60-day clock is suspended, for up to 180 days after you first identified the overpayment.

## Steps
1. **Quantify it:** review the affected claims (or a properly designed sample) to determine the amount.
2. **Report and refund** through your MAC's voluntary refund process (check, offset request or form).
3. **Document** the review, the findings and the refund.
4. **Fix the cause:** provider education, template changes or coding review.

## Tips
- Don't send corrected claims for each visit unless your MAC asks for that; most use a voluntary refund form.
- For large or systemic problems, talk to a healthcare attorney about whether a self-disclosure protocol applies.
