# Question
We hired a nurse practitioner. Can they see new patients and bill under their own NPI, and how much will Medicare pay?

# Answer
**Short answer:** yes. Nurse practitioners and physician assistants can bill Medicare for services within their state scope of practice, including new patient visits, under **their own NPI**. Medicare generally pays **85%** of the physician fee schedule amount.

## Before they bill
1. **Enroll** the NP in Medicare and reassign payments to your group.
2. **Credential** them with each commercial plan; some plans don't credential NPs, or limit what they can bill.
3. Check your **state's scope of practice** and any collaboration rules.

## Incident-to
Billing an NP's visit under a physician (to get 100%) is allowed only when all incident-to rules are met. New patients and new problems don't qualify.
