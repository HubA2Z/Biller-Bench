# Question
We billed 99397 for a Medicare patient's yearly physical and it was denied as not covered. The patient thought their yearly visit was free. What should we have billed?

# Answer
**Short answer:** Original Medicare **doesn't cover routine physical exams** (99381–99397). It covers the **Annual Wellness Visit** instead, a prevention planning visit, not a head-to-toe physical.

## Medicare's covered visits
- **Welcome to Medicare visit (IPPE), G0402:** once, within the first 12 months of Part B.
- **Initial AWV, G0438:** after the first 12 months of Part B, if the patient hasn't had an IPPE or AWV in the past 12 months.
- **Subsequent AWV, G0439:** once every 12 months after that.

Patients usually pay nothing for these when the provider accepts assignment.

## What to do
1. If the visit met AWV requirements, bill the AWV code instead.
2. If the patient wants a routine physical, tell them in advance it isn't covered. A voluntary ABN (modifier GX) documents that they were told.
3. If a problem was also addressed, a problem-oriented E/M with modifier 25 may be billable with documentation.
