# Question
Our surgeon saw a new patient and decided on surgery, which was done the next day. The E/M was denied as included in the surgery. Which modifier should we have used?

# Answer
**Short answer:** **modifier 57**. When an E/M results in the **decision to perform a major surgery** (90-day global period) and happens the **day before or the day of** the surgery, add modifier 57 so it's paid separately.

## 25 vs 57
- **57**: decision for surgery, for **major** procedures (90-day global).
- **25**: significant, separately identifiable E/M on the same day as a **minor** procedure (0 or 10-day global). The decision to do a minor procedure is part of the procedure itself.

## How to fix it
1. Confirm the procedure has a 90-day global period.
2. Confirm the note documents the decision for surgery.
3. Add modifier 57 to the E/M and send a corrected claim (or request a Medicare reopening).
