# Question
A primary care doctor referred a patient to our cardiologist for a consultation. We billed 99243 and Medicare denied it. Aren't consults payable?

# Answer
**Short answer:** **Medicare doesn't pay consultation codes** (99242–99245 for office, 99252–99255 for inpatient). Bill the regular E/M code instead.

## What to bill to Medicare
- **Office, new to your group's specialty:** new patient codes 99202–99205.
- **Office, established:** 99212–99215.
- **Hospital:** initial and subsequent hospital care codes.

## Commercial and Medicare Advantage plans
Many commercial plans still pay consultation codes, and others don't. Check each plan's policy before choosing the code family.

## How to fix it
Send a corrected claim (or request a Medicare reopening) with the right E/M code at the level the documentation supports.
