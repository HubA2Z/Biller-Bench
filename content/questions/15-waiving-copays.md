# Question
Some long-time patients ask us to skip their 20% coinsurance. Can we just write it off?

# Answer
**Short answer:** **not routinely.** Routinely waiving Medicare coinsurance or deductibles can violate federal law, including the Anti-Kickback Statute, and can misstate your actual charges.

## When a waiver is acceptable
- **Documented financial hardship**, decided **case by case** using a written policy (for example, income-based criteria).
- After **reasonable collection efforts** have failed.

## What to avoid
- Advertising "we accept insurance as payment in full."
- Waiving cost sharing for everyone, or as a way to attract patients.

## Good practice
1. Adopt a written financial hardship policy.
2. Keep documentation for each waiver.
3. Ask your healthcare attorney to review the policy.
