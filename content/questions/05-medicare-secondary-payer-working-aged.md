# Question
Our patient has Medicare and is still working, with group health insurance through their employer. Medicare denied our claim saying another payer may be primary (CO-22). Who do we bill first?

# Answer
**Short answer:** it depends on the size of the employer. For a patient who is 65 or older and covered by a **group health plan through their own or a spouse's current employment**, the group health plan is **primary when the employer has 20 or more employees**. With fewer than 20 employees, Medicare is usually primary.

## Other common situations
- **Disability (under 65):** the employer plan is primary when the employer has **100 or more employees**.
- **End-stage renal disease:** the group health plan is primary during a 30-month coordination period.
- **Retiree coverage:** Medicare is primary; the retiree plan is secondary.

## How to fix the claim
1. Ask the patient about current employment and the employer's size (or have them check with HR).
2. Bill the group health plan first, then send Medicare the secondary claim with the primary's payment details.
3. If Medicare's records are wrong (for example, the patient retired), the patient should report it to the Benefits Coordination & Recovery Center (BCRC).

## Prevent it
- Ask Medicare patients the Medicare Secondary Payer questions at registration and update them regularly.
