## What it means

X12 describes code 243 as **services not authorized by network/primary care providers**. Along with 242, it replaced the deactivated code 38.

## Common causes

1. **No referral from the member's primary care provider** for specialist care.
2. **The referral named a different specialist** or practice.
3. **The referral expired** or ran out of visits.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the plan's referral rules** and the member's assigned PCP.
2. **Ask the PCP's office for a referral;** some plans allow retroactive referrals.
3. **Resubmit with the referral information,** or appeal if the plan didn't require one for this service.

## Prevent it next time

- Verify referrals at scheduling for HMO and point-of-service plans.
- Track referral visit counts and expiry dates.
