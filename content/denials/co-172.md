## What it means

X12 describes code 172 as **payment is adjusted when performed/billed by a provider of this specialty**.

## Common causes

1. **Non-physician practitioner rates,** such as Medicare paying nurse practitioners and physician assistants at 85% of the physician fee schedule for most services.
2. **Specialty-based contract rates.**
3. **Wrong specialty** on the payer's record.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the rendering provider** and their specialty on file.
2. **Compare the payment** with the contract rate for that specialty.
3. **If the specialty is wrong,** update enrollment and ask the payer to reprocess.
4. **If the reduction is correct,** post it as a contractual adjustment.

## Prevent it next time

- Keep specialty data consistent across NPPES and payer enrollments.
