## What it means

X12 describes code 133 as **the disposition of this service line is pending further review**. It's used with group code **OA**, and the payer must reverse it when the line is finalized.

## Common causes

1. **Medical review or prepayment review** of the service.
2. **The payer is waiting** for information, such as records or another payer's response.
3. **Manual pricing** for unlisted or high-cost services.

## How to fix it

1. **Don't resubmit;** a new claim will usually be denied as a duplicate.
2. **Check claim status** on the payer portal for any records request.
3. **Send requested information quickly** and keep proof of submission.
4. **Follow up** if the line stays pending past the payer's usual processing time.

## Prevent it next time

- Attach documentation up front for services that often go to review.
- Watch for and answer records requests daily.
