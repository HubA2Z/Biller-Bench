## What it means

X12 describes code 55 as **procedure/treatment/drug is deemed experimental/investigational by the payer**.

## Common causes

1. **The payer's medical policy** lists the service as investigational.
2. **New technology** or a Category III CPT code.
3. **Off-label drug use** the plan doesn't cover.

## How to fix it

1. **Read the payer's medical policy** for the service and its effective date.
2. **Check coding:** make sure a covered service wasn't coded as an investigational one.
3. **Appeal with evidence** (peer-reviewed studies, specialty society guidelines, FDA status) if the service is accepted care.
4. **Bill the patient only** if a valid advance notice or waiver was signed under the plan's rules.

## Prevent it next time

- Check medical policy and get authorization before newer procedures.
- Get a signed advance notice when coverage is unlikely.
