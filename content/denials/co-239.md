## What it means

X12 describes code 239 as **claim spans eligible and ineligible periods of coverage; rebill separate claims**. One claim can't cover dates on both sides of an eligibility change.

## Common causes

1. **Coverage started or ended** in the middle of a billing period (common for monthly or recurring services).
2. **A plan change** mid-month.
3. **Span-dated claims** for DME rentals, therapy or care management.

## How to fix it

1. **Check eligibility dates** for the whole span.
2. **Split the claim** at the coverage change: one claim for covered dates to this payer, and the rest to the correct payer (or the patient if uncovered).
3. **Resubmit each claim separately.**

## Prevent it next time

- For span-dated services, check eligibility for every month in the span.
- Bill recurring services by calendar month when coverage may change.
