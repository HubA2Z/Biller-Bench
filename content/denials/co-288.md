## What it means

X12 describes code 288 as **referral absent**.

## Common causes

1. **No referral** was obtained before a specialist visit.
2. **The referral wasn't sent to or recorded by the payer.**
3. **The referral number** was missing from the claim.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the payer portal** for a referral on file.
2. **If one exists,** add the referral number or referring provider to the claim and resubmit.
3. **If none exists,** ask the PCP for one (retroactively if the plan allows), then resubmit.

## Prevent it next time

- Check referral requirements at scheduling for HMO and POS plans.
- Put the referring provider's NPI on the claim when the plan requires it.
