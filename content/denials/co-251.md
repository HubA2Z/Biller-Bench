## What it means

X12 describes code 251 as **the attachment/other documentation that was received was incomplete or deficient**.

## Common causes

1. **Missing pages,** signatures or dates.
2. **The wrong date of service** in the records.
3. **Missing supporting documents,** such as orders or test results.

## How to fix it

1. **Read the remark code** to see what was missing.
2. **Send the complete set** with the claim number and attachment reference.

## Prevent it next time

- Use a checklist for common attachment requests (notes, orders, results, signatures).
