## What it means

X12 describes code 149 as **lifetime benefit maximum has been reached for this service/benefit category**. Unlike code 35 (the whole plan), this limit applies to one category.

## Common causes

1. **Category-specific lifetime limits** (for example, some devices, fertility treatment or orthodontics).

## How to fix it

1. **Confirm the limit** and usage with the payer.
2. **Check whether a different benefit category** applies to the service.
3. **Bill other coverage or the patient** under your financial policy.

## Prevent it next time

- Verify category limits before planned services.
