## What it means

X12 describes code 22 as "this care may be covered by another payer per coordination of benefits." The payer's records show **other coverage that may be primary**, so it won't pay until the primary plan has processed the claim. Depending on the payer, it can appear with group code CO or OA.

For Medicare this usually means its records show a **Medicare Secondary Payer (MSP)** situation, such as an employer group health plan, liability, no-fault or workers' compensation.

## Common causes

1. **The patient has other coverage** that's primary, such as through current employment or a spouse.
2. **The payer's COB record is out of date** (for example, coverage that ended is still on file).
3. **An accident or injury claim** where liability, no-fault or workers' compensation may pay first.
4. **The secondary claim was sent without the primary payment information.** Medicare may add remark **MA04** (secondary payment can't be considered without the primary payer's information).
5. **The patient hasn't answered the plan's COB questionnaire.**

## How to fix it

1. **Check eligibility.** Payer portals and eligibility responses usually show the other coverage the payer has on file.
2. **Ask the patient** about all coverage and update your registration.
3. **If the other plan is primary,** bill it first, then send the secondary claim with the primary's payment and adjustment details.
4. **If the payer's record is wrong,** have the patient update it:

- **Medicare:** the patient reports changes in other coverage to the Benefits Coordination & Recovery Center (BCRC). If MSP no longer applies (for example, employment ended), providers can send termination requests to their Medicare claims office.
- **Commercial plans:** the patient usually updates COB with the plan by phone or online.

5. **Once the record is fixed,** resubmit the claim.

## Medicare vs. commercial payers

| | Medicare | Most commercial payers |
|---|---|---|
| Who keeps the other-coverage record | BCRC | The plan's COB unit |
| How it gets fixed | Patient reports changes to the BCRC; providers can request MSP termination through the MAC | Patient updates COB with the plan |
| Secondary claims | Include primary payment details | Include primary EOB or 835 data |

## Prevent it next time

- Ask every patient about other coverage at each visit, and use the Medicare MSP questions for Medicare patients.
- Scan and file both sides of every insurance card.
- Flag accident and injury visits so the right payer is billed first.
- Re-verify COB at the start of each year, when coverage often changes.
