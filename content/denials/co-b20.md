## What it means

X12 describes code B20 as **procedure/service was partially or fully furnished by another provider**.

## Common causes

1. **Global billing** when another provider billed part of the service (for example, a professional or technical component).
2. **Split surgical care** (surgery, pre-op or post-op care) without the right modifiers (54, 55, 56).
3. **Two providers billing the same service** for the same patient and date.

## How to fix it

1. **Ask the payer** which provider's claim caused the denial.
2. **Bill only your portion** with the right modifier (26, TC, 54, 55 or 56).
3. **If the other provider billed in error,** coordinate with them and ask the payer to reprocess.

## Prevent it next time

- Agree on billing responsibilities for shared services in advance.
- Use the correct split-care and component modifiers at charge entry.
