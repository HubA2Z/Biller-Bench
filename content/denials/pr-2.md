## What it means

After any deductible, the plan paid its percentage of the allowed amount and assigned the rest to the patient as **coinsurance**, for example 20%. Group code **PR** means the patient owes it.

## Common causes

1. **The plan design** includes coinsurance (common for procedures, imaging and specialist care).
2. **Medicare Part B,** where the patient generally owes 20% of the allowed amount after the deductible.
3. **Out-of-network services,** which usually carry higher coinsurance.

## How to fix it

1. **Confirm the coinsurance** is calculated on the allowed amount, not your charge.
2. **Bill the secondary payer** if there is one (Medigap, Medicaid or a second commercial plan). Medicare crossovers often happen automatically.
3. **Bill the patient** for what remains.
4. **If the service should have no cost sharing** (for example, an in-network preventive visit), ask the payer to reprocess it.

## Medicare vs. commercial payers

| | Medicare Part B | Most commercial payers |
|---|---|---|
| Typical coinsurance | 20% of the allowed amount for most services | Set by plan, often 10–50% |
| Secondary | Medigap or Medicaid may pay it | Second plan may pay it under COB |

## Prevent it next time

- Verify coinsurance percentage and secondary coverage before the visit.
- Collect estimated patient responsibility at check-in where your policy allows.
- Make sure crossover to Medigap and Medicaid is set up.
