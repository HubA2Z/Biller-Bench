## What it means

X12 describes code 203 as **discontinued or reduced service**. The payer paid less, or nothing, because the claim shows the service wasn't fully performed.

## Common causes

1. **Modifier 52** (reduced service) or **53** (discontinued procedure) on a professional claim.
2. **Modifiers 73 or 74** (procedure discontinued before or after anesthesia) on facility claims.
3. **A modifier left on by mistake** when the service was completed.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the documentation:** was the service reduced or stopped?
2. **If it was completed,** remove the modifier and send a corrected claim.
3. **If it was reduced or stopped,** make sure the right modifier was used and the documentation explains why. Payers often review these manually and may ask for notes.

## Prevent it next time

- Ask providers to document clearly when and why a service stopped.
- Review modifier 52, 53, 73 and 74 lines before submission.
