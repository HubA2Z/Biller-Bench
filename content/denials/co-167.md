## What it means

X12 describes code 167 as **this (these) diagnosis(es) is (are) not covered**.

## Common causes

1. **The diagnosis isn't on the payer's covered list** for the service (for Medicare, the LCD or NCD).
2. **The primary diagnosis** listed isn't the covered one, even though a covered one is documented.
3. **An unspecified code** when the policy lists specific codes.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the coverage policy** for covered diagnoses.
2. **Review the documentation** for a covered diagnosis that should have been coded or pointed to.
3. **Correct the claim** if so; otherwise adjust it off, or bill the patient only if a valid advance notice (ABN for Medicare) was signed.

## Prevent it next time

- Check covered diagnoses before ordering tests or procedures with coverage policies.
- Give an ABN when Medicare coverage is unlikely.
