## What it means

X12 describes code 96 as **non-covered charge(s)**, with at least one remark code required. You may see it as **CO-96** (provider liable) or **PR-96** (patient liable, for example when the plan allows billing the patient for non-covered services).

## Common causes

1. **A plan exclusion** (cosmetic services, certain supplies).
2. **A service not covered** in that setting or for that provider type.
3. **Coding that makes a covered service look non-covered.**

## How to fix it

1. **Read the remark code** for the reason.
2. **Check coding** in case a covered code applies.
3. **Appeal** if the service is covered under the patient's plan.
4. **Bill the patient** only when the group code is PR, or when a valid advance notice or waiver was signed under the plan's rules.

## Prevent it next time

- Check coverage for elective or unusual services ahead of time and get a signed advance notice.
