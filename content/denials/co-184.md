## What it means

X12 describes code 184 as **the prescribing/ordering provider is not eligible to prescribe/order the service billed**.

## Common causes

1. **The ordering provider isn't enrolled** with the payer.
2. **A provider type** not allowed to order that service (some DME and imaging have specific rules).
3. **Wrong ordering NPI or name** on the claim.

## How to fix it

1. **Match the claim to the signed order:** name and individual NPI.
2. **Check enrollment** (for Medicare, the CMS ordering and referring data).
3. **Correct the claim** or have the ordering provider enroll, then resubmit.

## Prevent it next time

- Check ordering providers' enrollment when you receive orders from outside practices.
