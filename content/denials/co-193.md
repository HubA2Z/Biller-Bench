## What it means

X12 describes code 193 as **original payment decision is being maintained; upon review, it was determined that this claim was processed properly**.

## Common causes

1. **An appeal or reconsideration** that didn't change the outcome.
2. **Missing or weak supporting documentation** in the appeal.
3. **A payer policy** that clearly applies to the service.

## How to fix it

1. **Read the decision letter** for the specific reason and the next appeal level and deadline.
2. **Strengthen the case:** add records, policy citations and a clear letter explaining why the service meets the payer's rules.
3. **File the next level** before its deadline. For Medicare, the levels after redetermination are reconsideration by a Qualified Independent Contractor (QIC), then higher levels.
4. **If the decision is correct,** adjust it off.

## Prevent it next time

- Include full documentation and the policy rule you rely on in the first appeal.
