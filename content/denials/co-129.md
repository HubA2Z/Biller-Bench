## What it means

X12 describes code 129 as **prior processing information appears incorrect**, and it requires at least one remark code.

## Common causes

1. **Secondary claim errors:** primary paid amount, adjustment codes or dates don't match the primary payer's remittance.
2. **Corrected claim errors:** the original claim number or frequency code is wrong.
3. **Totals that don't balance** (billed amount vs. primary paid plus adjustments).

## How to fix it

1. **Read the remark code** on the remittance.
2. **Compare the claim with the primary's remittance** or the original claim's details.
3. **Correct and resubmit.** For secondary claims, send the primary's adjustment group and reason codes exactly as received.

## Prevent it next time

- Post primary payments from the 835 file electronically, not by hand.
- Use the original payer claim number on every corrected claim.
