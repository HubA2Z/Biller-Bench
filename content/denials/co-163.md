## What it means

X12 describes code 163 as **attachment/other documentation referenced on the claim was not received**.

## Common causes

1. **The attachment wasn't sent** after the claim said it would be.
2. **The attachment control number** on the claim didn't match the fax or upload.
3. **It was sent to the wrong address or fax.**

## How to fix it

1. **Check the payer's instructions** for sending attachments (portal, fax cover sheet or electronic attachment).
2. **Send the documentation** with the matching attachment control number and claim number.
3. **Keep proof of sending,** then check status.

## Prevent it next time

- Send attachments the same day as the claim.
- Use the payer's cover sheet and the same control number on both.
