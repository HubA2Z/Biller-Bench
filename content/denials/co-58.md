## What it means

X12 describes code 58 as **treatment was deemed by the payer to have been rendered in an inappropriate or invalid place of service**.

## Common causes

1. **Wrong place of service code** on the claim.
2. **Site-of-service policies,** where a plan requires certain procedures to be done in an office or ambulatory surgery center instead of a hospital.
3. **Telehealth** billed with a place of service the payer doesn't allow.

## How to fix it

1. **Confirm the actual location** in the documentation.
2. **Correct the place of service** if it was wrong and resubmit.
3. **If the setting was right,** check the payer's site-of-service policy; appeal with medical reasons why that setting was needed.

## Prevent it next time

- Check site-of-service rules during prior authorization.
- Map place of service to the scheduling location automatically.
