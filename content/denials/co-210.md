## What it means

X12 describes code 210 as **payment adjusted because pre-certification/authorization not received in a timely fashion**.

## Common causes

1. **Late inpatient admission notification** (many plans require notice within a short time of admission).
2. **Retro authorization requested** after the payer's allowed window.
3. **A request faxed or submitted** but not recorded by the payer.

## How to fix it

1. **Check when the request was sent** and when the payer received it.
2. **Appeal with proof** of timely submission (portal confirmation, fax log or call reference).
3. **If the delay was due to an emergency,** explain it in the appeal; many plans have exceptions.

## Prevent it next time

- Know each plan's notification deadlines for admissions and urgent procedures.
- Save confirmation of every authorization request.
