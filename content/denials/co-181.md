## What it means

X12 describes code 181 as **procedure code was invalid on the date of service**.

## Common causes

1. **A deleted code** still in your charge master after the annual update.
2. **A new code used too early,** before its effective date.
3. **Payer systems** that haven't loaded a new code yet.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the code's effective and end dates** for the date of service.
2. **Replace it with the valid code** and resubmit.
3. **If the code was valid and the payer's system is behind,** ask the payer to reprocess.

## Prevent it next time

- Update your charge master before January 1 and at each quarterly HCPCS update.
- Remove deleted codes from templates and superbills.
