## What it means

X12 describes code 242 as **services not provided by network/primary care providers**. It replaced the deactivated code 38.

## Common causes

1. **An out-of-network provider** saw an HMO or EPO member for non-emergency care.
2. **The provider's network participation** with that specific plan isn't loaded correctly.
3. **The member chose a different primary care provider** than the one on file with the plan.

## How to fix it

1. **Confirm your network status** with that specific plan (not just the insurer).
2. **If you're in network,** ask the payer to fix the provider record and reprocess.
3. **If you're out of network,** check whether the plan allows exceptions (emergencies, network gaps, continuity of care) and appeal if one applies.
4. **Follow the plan's and your state's rules** on billing the patient.

## Prevent it next time

- Check network participation for the patient's exact plan before scheduling.
- Confirm the member's assigned primary care provider.
