## What it means

X12 describes code 177 as **patient has not met the required eligibility requirements**. The patient may be enrolled, but didn't meet a requirement (such as a program category, age or other condition) that the service needs.

## Common causes

1. **Medicaid eligibility category** doesn't cover this service (for example, a limited-benefit category).
2. **Eligibility pending** or recently changed on the date of service.
3. **A program-specific requirement** (such as a waiver program) wasn't met.

## How to fix it

1. **Check eligibility details**, including the program or benefit category, for the date of service.
2. **If the patient became eligible retroactively,** resubmit once the payer's records are updated.
3. **If another program or plan covers the service,** bill that one.
4. **If the patient isn't eligible,** follow your financial policy and any program rules about billing the patient.

## Prevent it next time

- Look at the eligibility category, not only "active," for Medicaid patients.
- Recheck eligibility monthly for patients in ongoing treatment.
