## What it means

X12 describes code 285 as **appeal procedures not followed**.

## Common causes

1. **Wrong form or channel,** such as a corrected claim sent instead of an appeal, or the reverse.
2. **Missing required items** like a signed form, the original claim number or the remittance.
3. **Skipping a level,** such as requesting a second-level appeal before the first.

## How to fix it

1. **Read the payer's appeal instructions** (provider manual or portal).
2. **Refile correctly** with every required item.
3. **Watch the deadline;** a misfiled appeal usually doesn't extend it.

## Prevent it next time

- Keep an appeal checklist per payer.
- Use payer portals for appeals where available; they enforce required fields.
