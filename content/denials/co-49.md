## What it means

X12 describes code 49 as **this is a non-covered service because it is a routine/preventive exam or a diagnostic/screening procedure done in conjunction with a routine/preventive exam**.

## Common causes

1. **A routine physical billed to Medicare,** which doesn't cover routine physical exams.
2. **A preventive visit** beyond the plan's frequency limit.
3. **Screening tests** done with a routine exam that the plan doesn't cover.

## How to fix it

1. **For Medicare,** check whether the visit qualifies as the Initial Preventive Physical Examination (IPPE) or Annual Wellness Visit (AWV), which are covered under their own codes.
2. **For commercial plans,** check the preventive benefit and frequency.
3. **If a separate problem was addressed,** a problem-oriented visit may be billable with the right modifier and documentation.
4. **Otherwise,** bill the patient if they were told in advance, under the plan's rules.

## Prevent it next time

- Verify preventive benefits and last-visit dates before scheduling.
- Tell Medicare patients in advance that routine physicals aren't covered.
