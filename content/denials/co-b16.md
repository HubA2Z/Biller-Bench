## What it means

X12 describes code B16 as **'New Patient' qualifications were not met**. Under CPT and Medicare rules, a new patient is one who hasn't received professional services from the physician, or another physician of the **exact same specialty and subspecialty in the same group**, in the **past three years**.

## Common causes

1. **The patient was seen within three years** by the same provider or a same-specialty colleague in the group.
2. **Group or specialty enrollment** that makes two providers look like the same specialty to the payer.
3. **A provider who changed groups** and brought patients along (payers may still count prior visits).

## How to fix it

1. **Check visit history** for the past three years across the group.
2. **If the patient was established,** send a corrected claim with the established-patient code at the right level.
3. **If the prior visit was a different specialty or more than three years ago,** appeal with that information.

## Prevent it next time

- Check visit history at scheduling and flag returning patients.
- Keep provider specialty data accurate with each payer.
