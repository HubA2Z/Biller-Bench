## What it means

X12 describes code 204 as **this service/equipment/drug is not covered under the patient's current benefit plan**. You may see **PR-204** (patient responsible) or **CO-204** (provider liable under the contract).

## Common causes

1. **A plan exclusion** (for example, certain cosmetic, vision, hearing or dental services under a medical plan).
2. **A benefit carved out** to another plan or vendor.
3. **The wrong code** that maps to an excluded benefit.

## How to fix it

1. **Confirm the exclusion** in the plan's benefits.
2. **Check whether another plan** (dental, vision or a carve-out vendor) covers it.
3. **Check coding** for a covered alternative that matches the documentation.
4. **Bill the patient** when the group code is PR.

## Prevent it next time

- Check benefits for elective services and collect or estimate in advance.
