## What it means

X12 describes code 150 as **payer deems the information submitted does not support this level of service**.

## Common causes

1. **High-level E/M visits** (for example, the highest office visit level) where the note doesn't show the medical decision making or time required.
2. **Payer downcoding policies** applied automatically to high-level visits.
3. **Time-based billing** without total time documented.

## How to fix it

1. **Score the visit** against current E/M guidelines (medical decision making or total time on the date of the encounter).
2. **If the level is supported,** appeal with the note and a short summary of how it meets the level.
3. **If not,** accept the lower level or send a corrected claim.

## Prevent it next time

- Train providers on documenting medical decision making and total time.
- Audit a sample of high-level visits every quarter.
