## What it means

X12 describes code 110 as **billing date predates service date**. A claim can't be billed before the service is performed.

## Common causes

1. **A typo in the date of service** (often the year).
2. **Span-dated services** (such as monthly care management) billed before the period ended.
3. **Pre-billing** recurring services in advance.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the date of service** against the documentation.
2. **For span or monthly services,** wait until the service period ends before billing.
3. **Correct the claim and resubmit.**

## Prevent it next time

- Block future dates of service in your billing system.
- Bill monthly services only after the month closes.
