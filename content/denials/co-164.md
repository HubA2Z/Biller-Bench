## What it means

X12 describes code 164 as **attachment/other documentation referenced on the claim was not received in a timely fashion**.

## Common causes

1. **Records sent after the payer's deadline** following the claim or request.
2. **Records requests** that weren't noticed in time.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the payer's process:** some will reconsider the claim if you resubmit with the records; others require an appeal.
2. **Send the documentation** with the claim number and any reference number.
3. **Keep proof** of the date you sent it.

## Prevent it next time

- Track every records request and its due date.
- Send attachments with the claim whenever you know they'll be needed.
