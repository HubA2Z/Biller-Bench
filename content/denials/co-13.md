## What it means

X12 describes code 13 as **the date of death precedes the date of service**. The payer won't pay for a service dated after it believes the patient died.

## Common causes

1. **A typo in the date of service.**
2. **The wrong patient** was selected in the billing system.
3. **The payer's death record is wrong** (for example, an incorrect Social Security death report).
4. **Services after death,** such as a pronouncement billed on the wrong date.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the date of service** against the visit note.
2. **Confirm the right patient** is on the claim.
3. **If the claim is correct and the payer's record is wrong,** the patient or family needs to correct it with the payer or the Social Security Administration; then resubmit.

## Prevent it next time

- Check dates of service at charge entry.
- Use two identifiers when selecting patients in the billing system.
