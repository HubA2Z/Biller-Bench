## What it means

X12 describes code 19 as **this is a work-related injury/illness and thus the liability of the Worker's Compensation Carrier**.

## Common causes

1. **The patient said the injury happened at work,** or the diagnosis or accident indicator suggests it.
2. **The plan has an open workers' comp record** for the patient.
3. **The accident indicator** on the claim was marked "employment related."

## How to fix it

1. **Ask the patient** whether the injury was work-related and get the employer, carrier, claim number and adjuster.
2. **Bill the workers' comp carrier** following your state's rules and forms.
3. **If it wasn't work-related,** correct the accident indicator and ask the health plan to reprocess; the patient may need to confirm this with the plan.
4. **If workers' comp denied the claim,** send that denial to the health plan with the claim.

## Prevent it next time

- Ask about work injuries at intake and record accident details.
- Keep workers' comp and health plan accounts separate.
