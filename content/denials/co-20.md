## What it means

X12 describes code 20 as **this injury/illness is covered by the liability carrier**.

## Common causes

1. **An accident** (car accident, slip and fall) where another party may be liable.
2. **The plan's questionnaire** about the accident wasn't answered.
3. **The accident indicator** on the claim suggested third-party liability.

## How to fix it

1. **Get accident details:** date, type, other party's insurer and claim number, and any attorney.
2. **Bill the liability carrier** if appropriate, or follow your policy for liability cases.
3. **If the plan should pay** (for example, no liability coverage applies), have the patient complete the plan's accident questionnaire, then ask for reprocessing.
4. **For Medicare patients,** Medicare may pay conditionally in some liability cases; follow Medicare Secondary Payer rules.

## Prevent it next time

- Ask about accidents at intake and record the details.
- Send the plan's accident questionnaire to the patient right away.
