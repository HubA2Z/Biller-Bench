## What it means

The payer received the claim **after the time limit for filing** had passed, so it denied it without reviewing the service.

- **Medicare:** claims must be filed no later than **one calendar year after the date of service** (42 CFR 424.44).
- **Commercial and Medicare Advantage plans:** the deadline is set by your contract or the plan's provider manual, and is often much shorter than a year.

## Common causes

1. **The claim was rejected at the clearinghouse** and nobody worked the rejection.
2. **Wrong payer first.** The claim went to a payer that wasn't responsible, and by the time it reached the right one the deadline had passed.
3. **Secondary claims sent late** after a slow primary payment.
4. **Corrected claims** sent after the payer's corrected-claim deadline.
5. **Charges entered late** after the visit.

## How to fix it

1. **Confirm the dates.** Compare the date of service with the date the payer first received the claim.
2. **Find proof of timely filing.** Clearinghouse acceptance reports (999 and 277CA), payer acknowledgments, or an earlier denial from the same payer can show the claim was sent in time.
3. **Commercial payers:** appeal with that proof. Most payers accept an electronic acceptance report from the payer or clearinghouse.
4. **Medicare:** check whether an exception applies. The deadline can be extended to the end of the sixth calendar month after you're notified when:

- a Medicare contractor or CMS error caused the delay;
- the patient was made retroactively entitled to Medicare;
- the patient was retroactively disenrolled from a Medicare Advantage plan or PACE and the plan recovered its payment.

5. **If no proof or exception exists,** the denial stands. Adjust it off. Don't bill the patient.

## Medicare vs. commercial payers

| | Medicare | Most commercial payers |
|---|---|---|
| Deadline | 1 calendar year after the date of service | Set by contract; check the provider manual |
| Exceptions | Contractor error, retroactive entitlement, retroactive MA or PACE disenrollment | Usually proof of timely filing only |
| Fix | Show an exception applies | Appeal with proof of timely submission |

## Prevent it next time

- Keep a payer-by-payer table of filing deadlines, including corrected-claim and secondary-claim deadlines.
- Work clearinghouse rejections every day; a rejected claim was never received.
- Enter charges within a few days of the visit.
- Run a weekly report of unbilled visits and unpaid claims older than 30 days.
