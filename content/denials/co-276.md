## What it means

X12 describes code 276 as **services denied by the prior payer(s) are not covered by this payer**.

## Common causes

1. **A non-covered service** under both plans.
2. **A fixable primary denial** (missing information, authorization or coding) that carried through to the secondary.

## How to fix it

1. **Review the primary's denial reason.**
2. **If it's fixable,** fix it with the primary, then resubmit the secondary with the new primary remittance.
3. **If the service isn't covered by either plan,** follow your financial policy and any advance notice the patient signed.

## Prevent it next time

- Check coverage with both plans for planned services.
