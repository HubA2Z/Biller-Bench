## What it means

X12 describes code 227 as **information requested from the patient/insured/responsible party was not provided or was insufficient/incomplete**.

## Common causes

1. **An unanswered COB questionnaire** (other insurance).
2. **An unanswered accident or injury questionnaire.**
3. **Student status or dependent information** the plan requested.

## How to fix it

1. **Tell the patient** what the plan needs and how to respond (phone, portal or form).
2. **Once the patient responds,** ask the payer to reprocess the claim.
3. **Follow up;** reprocessing often doesn't happen automatically.

## Prevent it next time

- Remind patients at check-in to answer plan questionnaires.
