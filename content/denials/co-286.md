## What it means

X12 describes code 286 as **appeal time limits not met**.

## Common causes

1. **Late appeal** after the payer's window.
2. **Time spent on a corrected claim** when the payer needed an appeal.
3. **Misfiled appeals** that were rejected and resent too late.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the dates:** when the denial was received and when the appeal was sent.
2. **If you filed in time,** resubmit with proof (portal confirmation, certified mail receipt or fax log).
3. **If you missed it for a reason the payer accepts** (Medicare calls this good cause), explain it in writing.
4. **Otherwise,** the decision stands; adjust it off.

## Medicare vs. commercial payers

| | Medicare | Most commercial payers |
|---|---|---|
| First-level appeal deadline | Redetermination within 120 days of receiving the initial determination | Set by contract or plan, often 60 to 180 days |
| Late filing | Good-cause extension may be granted | Varies |

## Prevent it next time

- Log every denial with its appeal deadline the day it posts.
- Work appeals oldest-deadline first.
