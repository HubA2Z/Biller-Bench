## What it means

X12 describes code 228 as **denied for failure of this provider, another provider or the subscriber to supply requested information to a previous payer for their adjudication**.

## Common causes

1. **The primary payer requested records** from the provider that weren't sent.
2. **The primary asked the patient** for COB or accident information that wasn't returned.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the primary's remittance** for the information request.
2. **Send what was requested** (records from you, or ask the patient to respond).
3. **When the primary processes the claim,** resubmit to this payer with the primary's remittance.

## Prevent it next time

- Track information requests from primary payers as closely as denials.
