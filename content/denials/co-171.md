## What it means

X12 describes code 171 as **payment is denied when performed/billed by this type of provider in this type of facility**.

## Common causes

1. **Wrong place of service** on the claim.
2. **Facility rules** where the facility, not the provider, must bill the service (for example, services included in a facility's payment).
3. **Provider type limits** in certain settings, such as skilled nursing or hospital outpatient.

## How to fix it

1. **Confirm the place of service** and correct it if wrong.
2. **Check whether the facility should bill** the service instead.
3. **Resubmit or adjust off** based on the payer's rule.

## Prevent it next time

- Map billing responsibilities with each facility you work in.
