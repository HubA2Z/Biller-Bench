## What it means

X12 describes code 296 as **precertification/authorization/notification/pre-treatment number may be valid but does not apply to the provider**.

## Common causes

1. **The authorization named another provider** in the group, or a different location or facility.
2. **The rendering provider changed** after the authorization was issued.
3. **The group NPI vs. individual NPI** didn't match the authorization.

## How to fix it

1. **Check which provider and location** the authorization lists.
2. **Ask the payer to update it** to the provider who performed the service.
3. **Resubmit,** or appeal with proof of the update.

## Prevent it next time

- Request authorizations under the provider and location that will perform the service.
- Update authorizations when schedules change.
