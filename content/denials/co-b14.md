## What it means

X12 describes code B14 as **only one visit or consultation per physician per day is covered**. Many payers apply this to providers in the same group and specialty too.

## Common causes

1. **Two E/M visits** billed for the same patient on the same day by the same provider or group.
2. **A morning and afternoon visit** for related problems.
3. **Two providers of the same specialty** in the same group seeing the patient the same day.

## How to fix it

1. **Check whether the visits were related.** Medicare generally expects related same-day visits to be combined into one E/M level reflecting the total work.
2. **If combined,** send a corrected claim with one E/M at the right level.
3. **If the second visit was separate and unrelated,** check the payer's policy for a modifier (such as 25) and appeal with documentation.

## Prevent it next time

- Flag same-day visits by the same group at charge review.
- Document why a second same-day visit was needed.
