## What it means

X12 describes code 206 as **National Provider Identifier - missing**.

## Common causes

1. **No rendering NPI** on a group claim.
2. **No referring or ordering NPI** when the service requires one.
3. **No facility or service location NPI** when required.

## How to fix it

1. **Find which NPI is missing** (the remark code usually says).
2. **Add it** to the claim and the provider setup in your billing system.
3. **Resubmit.**

## Prevent it next time

- Make NPI fields required in your billing system for every provider type.
