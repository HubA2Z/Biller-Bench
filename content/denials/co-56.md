## What it means

X12 describes code 56 as **procedure/treatment has not been deemed 'proven to be effective' by the payer**.

## Common causes

1. **The diagnosis isn't one** the policy considers proven for the service.
2. **The patient didn't meet policy criteria,** such as failing conservative treatment first.
3. **The payer's policy** lags behind newer evidence.

## How to fix it

1. **Read the medical policy criteria.**
2. **Check the documentation** shows the patient met them.
3. **Appeal with records and evidence;** ask for a peer-to-peer review where available.

## Prevent it next time

- Get authorization for services with restrictive policies.
- Document prior treatments and why they failed.
