## What it means

X12 describes code B12 as **services not documented in patient's medical records**.

## Common causes

1. **Missing or unsigned notes.**
2. **The wrong date of service** in the record.
3. **Services billed** that aren't mentioned in the note.

## How to fix it

1. **Find the signed note** for the date of service.
2. **Appeal with complete records** if documentation exists.
3. **If the service wasn't documented,** adjust it off. Late entries added after a denial are viewed skeptically and must follow proper amendment rules.

## Prevent it next time

- Only bill from signed notes.
- Audit charges against documentation regularly.
