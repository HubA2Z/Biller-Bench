## What it means

X12 describes code 252 as **an attachment/other documentation is required to adjudicate this claim/service**.

## Common causes

1. **Unlisted codes** that need a procedure note.
2. **Modifier-based services** (for example, 22 or 52) that the payer reviews manually.
3. **High-cost drugs or supplies** that need an invoice.

## How to fix it

1. **Read the remark code** for the documents needed.
2. **Send the documentation** through the payer's attachment process with the claim reference.
3. **Check status** after sending.

## Prevent it next time

- Send documentation with the claim for services that always need it.
