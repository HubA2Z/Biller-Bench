## What it means

X12 describes code B9 as **patient is enrolled in a hospice**. Under the Medicare hospice benefit, care related to the terminal illness and related conditions is paid through the hospice.

## Common causes

1. **The service was related** to the terminal illness and should be billed to (and paid by) the hospice.
2. **An unrelated service** billed without the modifier that shows it's unrelated.
3. **Attending physician services** billed without the attending modifier.

## How to fix it

1. **Check hospice election dates** in eligibility.
2. **If the service was related to the terminal illness,** coordinate payment with the hospice under its arrangement.
3. **If unrelated,** add modifier **GW** (service not related to the hospice patient's terminal condition) with a supporting diagnosis, and resubmit.
4. **If you're the attending physician** not employed by the hospice, add modifier **GV**.

## Prevent it next time

- Check hospice status during eligibility verification.
- Agree with local hospices on how related services are billed.
