## What it means

X12 describes code 12 as **the diagnosis is inconsistent with the provider type**. The payer limits some diagnoses (or services for them) to certain provider types or specialties.

## Common causes

1. **Wrong rendering provider** on the claim.
2. **Taxonomy code** on the claim doesn't match the provider's specialty on file with the payer.
3. **Payer policy** limits certain services to specific specialties (for example, behavioral health carve-outs).

## How to fix it

1. **Confirm who actually rendered the service** and correct the rendering NPI.
2. **Check the taxonomy** against the provider's enrollment with the payer.
3. **Read the payer's policy** for the service; if another specialty or carve-out vendor must bill it, route it there.

## Prevent it next time

- Keep each provider's specialty and taxonomy consistent across NPPES, payer enrollment and claims.
