## What it means

X12 describes code 200 as **expenses incurred during lapse in coverage**. The patient was covered before and after, but not on the date of service.

## Common causes

1. **Unpaid premium** caused a suspension that later lifted.
2. **Medicaid redetermination gap** when paperwork wasn't processed in time.
3. **A job change** with a gap between employer plans.

## How to fix it

1. **Check eligibility for the exact date** and ask the payer whether reinstatement is pending.
2. **If coverage is reinstated retroactively,** resubmit the claim once the payer's records are updated.
3. **Check for other coverage** during the gap, such as COBRA, Medicaid or a spouse's plan.
4. **If there was no coverage,** bill the patient under your financial policy.

## Prevent it next time

- Verify eligibility before every visit, especially for ongoing treatment.
- Remind Medicaid patients about renewal dates.
