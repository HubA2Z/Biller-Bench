## What it means

X12 describes code 152 as **payer deems the information submitted does not support this length of service**.

## Common causes

1. **Time-based codes** where the documented time doesn't meet the code's threshold.
2. **Inpatient or facility days** beyond what the payer approved or what the record supports.
3. **Start and stop times** missing from the note.

## How to fix it

1. **Compare documented time or days** with the claim.
2. **Correct the code or units** if they were wrong.
3. **Appeal with records** if the length was supported and necessary.

## Prevent it next time

- Document start and stop times, or total time, for time-based services.
- Track approved days for inpatient and facility stays.
