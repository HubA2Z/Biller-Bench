## What it means

X12 describes code 166 as **services submitted after this payer's responsibility for processing claims under this plan ended**. The payer no longer processes claims for this plan or group, even if coverage existed on the date of service.

## Common causes

1. **The employer changed plans or claims administrators** and the old one has stopped processing claims.
2. **A run-out period ended:** some plans accept old claims only for a limited time after they terminate.
3. **A Medicaid or managed care contract ended** and a new organization took over.

## How to fix it

1. **Contact the payer** to ask who now handles claims for that plan.
2. **Send the claim to the new administrator** with the date of service and proof of coverage.
3. **If you sent the claim within the run-out period,** appeal with your proof of timely submission.

## Prevent it next time

- Bill promptly, especially for patients whose employer plan is changing.
- Watch payer notices about plan terminations and run-out deadlines.
