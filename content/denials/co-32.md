## What it means

X12 describes code 32 as **our records indicate the patient is not an eligible dependent**. The subscriber has coverage, but this patient isn't covered as a dependent on it.

## Common causes

1. **The dependent was never added** to the policy.
2. **The dependent aged out** (commercial plans generally cover children up to age 26).
3. **Student or disability status** wasn't updated where the plan requires it.
4. **The claim lists the wrong subscriber,** for example the other parent's policy.

## How to fix it

1. **Check eligibility** for the dependent specifically, not only the subscriber.
2. **Ask the family** which parent's plan covers the child, and whether coordination of benefits applies.
3. **If the dependent should be covered,** the subscriber must contact the plan or employer to fix enrollment; then resubmit.
4. **Bill the correct plan,** or the family under your financial policy if there's no coverage.

## Prevent it next time

- Verify dependents individually at registration.
- Collect both parents' insurance for children and confirm which is primary.
