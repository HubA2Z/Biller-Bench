## What it means

X12 describes code 303 as **prior payer's (or payers') patient responsibility (deductible, coinsurance, co-payment) is not covered for Qualified Medicare and Medicaid Beneficiaries**, used only with group code **CO**.

Federal rules don't allow Medicare providers to bill patients in the **QMB program** for Medicare deductibles, coinsurance or copays. Medicaid may pay some or all of that cost sharing, or none of it, depending on your state's rules.

## Common causes

1. **The patient is a QMB or dual-eligible beneficiary** and Medicaid paid nothing toward the cost sharing.
2. **State Medicaid limits** on what it pays for Medicare cost sharing.

## How to fix it

1. **Confirm the patient's QMB or Medicaid status** for the date of service.
2. **Make sure the crossover claim** reached Medicaid and processed correctly.
3. **Adjust off the remaining balance.** Don't send a statement to the patient for it.

## Prevent it next time

- Flag QMB and dual-eligible patients in registration so statements are blocked.
- Check Medicare eligibility responses, which can show QMB status.
