## What it means

X12 describes code 119 as **benefit maximum for this time period or occurrence has been reached**. It may appear as **CO-119** or **PR-119** depending on the payer and your contract.

## Common causes

1. **Annual visit limits** for therapy, chiropractic, behavioral health or similar services.
2. **Visits at other providers** counting toward the same limit.
3. **Frequency limits** for certain equipment or tests.

## How to fix it

1. **Ask the payer** for the limit and the visits already used (including at other providers).
2. **Check for exceptions** or an extension process for medically necessary care.
3. **Appeal with documentation** if an exception applies.
4. **Bill the patient** only if the group code is PR or your contract and the plan allow it.

## Prevent it next time

- Check remaining visits during verification and track usage.
