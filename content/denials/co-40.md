## What it means

X12 describes code 40 as **charges do not meet qualifications for emergent or urgent care**. The payer reviewed the claim as an emergency or urgent visit and decided it didn't qualify.

## Common causes

1. **The final diagnosis looked minor,** even though the symptoms at arrival were serious.
2. **Emergency place of service or codes** used for a non-emergency visit.
3. **Plan rules** for urgent care settings weren't met.

## How to fix it

1. **Review the record** for the presenting symptoms and why they suggested an emergency.
2. **Make sure symptom codes** for the presenting complaint are on the claim when appropriate.
3. **Appeal with the records,** citing the plan's emergency definition. Many plans and federal rules use a "prudent layperson" standard based on symptoms, not the final diagnosis.

## Prevent it next time

- Code presenting symptoms along with the final diagnosis when guidelines allow.
- Document why the patient sought emergency care.
