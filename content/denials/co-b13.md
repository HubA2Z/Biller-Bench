## What it means

X12 describes code B13 as **previously paid; payment for this claim/service may have been provided in a previous payment**.

## Common causes

1. **The claim was resubmitted** after the original was paid.
2. **The payment was misposted** in your system or sent to another tax ID or provider number.
3. **A corrected claim** submitted as a new claim.

## How to fix it

1. **Search your remittances** for the original payment by patient, date of service and amount.
2. **If you find it,** post it correctly and close the duplicate.
3. **If you can't find it,** ask the payer for the payment details (check or EFT number and date) and trace it.
4. **If you meant to correct the claim,** use the payer's corrected-claim process.

## Prevent it next time

- Check claim status before resubmitting.
- Post ERAs electronically to avoid lost payments.
