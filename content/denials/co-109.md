## What it means

X12 describes code 109 as **claim or service not covered by this payer or contractor; you must send it to the correct payer or contractor**. The claim isn't denied on its merits; it's at the wrong place.

## Common causes

1. **The patient is in a Medicare Advantage or Medicaid managed care plan,** but the claim went to Original Medicare or fee-for-service Medicaid.
2. **Wrong Medicare contractor,** such as a Part B claim sent to a MAC outside your jurisdiction, or a DME item billed to the A/B MAC instead of the DME MAC.
3. **A carved-out benefit,** such as behavioral health, vision or lab, handled by a separate company.
4. **The wrong payer ID** was selected in the clearinghouse.

## How to fix it

1. **Check eligibility** to see which plan or contractor covers this patient and service.
2. **For carve-outs,** look for the vendor named on the card or in the eligibility response.
3. **Submit to the correct payer** and watch its filing deadline; the clock usually runs from the date of service.
4. **Correct the payer setup** in your billing system so future claims route correctly.

## Medicare vs. commercial payers

| | Medicare | Most commercial payers |
|---|---|---|
| Usual cause | Medicare Advantage enrollment, wrong MAC or DME MAC | Carve-out vendor or wrong payer ID |

## Prevent it next time

- Verify eligibility, including Medicare Advantage enrollment, at every visit.
- Keep a list of each plan's carve-out vendors.
- Audit clearinghouse payer IDs when you add a new plan.
