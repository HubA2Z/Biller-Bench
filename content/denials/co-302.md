## What it means

X12 describes code 302 as **precertification/notification/authorization/pre-treatment time limit has expired**.

## Common causes

1. **The service was rescheduled** to after the authorization end date.
2. **Long courses of care** that outlasted the approval period.
3. **Wrong dates** on the claim or in the payer's record.

## How to fix it

1. **Compare the date of service** with the authorization's start and end dates.
2. **If the dates were within range,** appeal with the authorization details.
3. **If expired,** ask the payer for an extension or a new authorization (retroactively if allowed) and resubmit.

## Prevent it next time

- Re-check authorization dates when rescheduling.
- Track expiry dates and request renewals early.
