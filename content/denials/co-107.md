## What it means

X12 describes code 107 as **the related or qualifying claim/service was not identified on this claim**. The billed service is only payable together with another service the payer can't see.

## Common causes

1. **Add-on code billed without its primary code,** or the primary was denied.
2. **A professional claim** that depends on a facility claim, or the reverse, that hasn't been processed.
3. **A claim split** across two submissions, separating related services.

## How to fix it

1. **Check that the qualifying service** was billed and paid by this payer.
2. **If the primary was denied,** fix that denial first.
3. **Resubmit the dependent service** on the same claim as the qualifying service where the payer requires it.

## Prevent it next time

- Keep add-on codes on the same claim as their primary codes.
- Hold dependent claims until the qualifying service is billed.
