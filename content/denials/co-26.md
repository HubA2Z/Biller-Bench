## What it means

X12 describes code 26 as **expenses incurred prior to coverage**. The payer's records show the policy's effective date is **after** the date of service.

## Common causes

1. **The patient gave a new card** for a plan that started after the visit.
2. **Coverage was pending** (for example, a new job or Marketplace enrollment) on the date of service.
3. **The payer has the wrong effective date** on file.

## How to fix it

1. **Check eligibility for the exact date of service.**
2. **Find the plan that was active then** and bill it.
3. **If the payer's effective date is wrong,** ask the patient or employer to have it corrected, then resubmit.
4. **If retroactive coverage was granted** (for example, Medicaid), bill once the coverage is in the payer's system.
5. **If no coverage existed,** bill the patient under your financial policy.

## Prevent it next time

- Verify eligibility before every visit and save the response.
- Ask new patients when their coverage began.
