## What it means

X12 describes code 198 as **precertification/notification/authorization/pre-treatment exceeded**.

## Common causes

1. **More visits or units** than approved (common with therapy, behavioral health and infusions).
2. **Dates of service after the authorization ended.**
3. **A different or additional code** than the one approved.

## How to fix it

1. **Compare the claim with the authorization:** visits, units, codes and dates.
2. **Request an extension or update** from the payer; some allow retro updates for a short time.
3. **Resubmit with the updated authorization,** or appeal with documentation of why more care was needed.

## Prevent it next time

- Track visits used against visits approved for each authorization.
- Request extensions before the last approved visit.
