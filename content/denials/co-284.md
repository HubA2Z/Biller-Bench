## What it means

X12 describes code 284 as **precertification/authorization/notification/pre-treatment number may be valid but does not apply to the billed services**. Medicare and other payers may add remark **N54** (claim information inconsistent with authorized services).

## Common causes

1. **The procedure performed changed** from what was authorized (for example, a different or additional code).
2. **Authorization for a different service type** (such as office vs. outpatient facility).
3. **The wrong authorization number** for this episode.

## How to fix it

1. **Compare the billed codes** with the authorized codes.
2. **Ask the payer to update the authorization** to match what was done; many allow this shortly after the service.
3. **Resubmit with the updated authorization,** or appeal with documentation of why the change was medically needed.

## Prevent it next time

- Authorize likely code variations in advance when possible.
- Update authorizations promptly when a procedure changes.
