## What it means

X12 describes code 185 as **the rendering provider is not eligible to perform the service billed**.

## Common causes

1. **The provider isn't credentialed** with the payer yet, or credentialing is pending.
2. **The enrollment effective date** is after the date of service.
3. **The provider isn't linked** to the group's tax ID or location with the payer.
4. **The service is outside the provider's scope** under the payer's rules.

## How to fix it

1. **Check credentialing status** and effective dates with the payer.
2. **If enrollment is pending,** ask whether the payer will make it retroactive and hold claims until it's approved.
3. **Fix group linkage** so the provider is attached to the right tax ID and location.
4. **Resubmit** once enrollment is active for the date of service.

## Prevent it next time

- Start credentialing well before a new provider's first patient day.
- Track enrollment effective dates by payer.
