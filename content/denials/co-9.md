## What it means

X12 describes code 9 as **the diagnosis is inconsistent with the patient's age**. ICD-10-CM has codes intended for specific ages, such as newborn (perinatal) codes and some pediatric or adult conditions.

## Common causes

1. **Wrong birth date** in registration.
2. **A perinatal or newborn code** used after the newborn period it applies to, or on the mother's claim.
3. **The wrong patient** selected.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the birth date** against the eligibility response.
2. **Review the code's guidelines** in ICD-10-CM and choose a code valid for the patient's age.
3. **Correct the claim and resubmit.**

## Prevent it next time

- Add age edits for diagnosis codes in your claim scrubber.
- Verify demographics at every visit.
