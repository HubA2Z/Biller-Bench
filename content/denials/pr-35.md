## What it means

X12 describes code 35 as **lifetime benefit maximum has been reached**.

## Common causes

1. **Lifetime limits** on certain benefits (some plans still have them for non-essential benefits such as some fertility or orthodontic care).
2. **Plans not subject to ACA rules** on lifetime limits for essential health benefits.

## How to fix it

1. **Confirm the limit** and how much has been used with the payer.
2. **Check other coverage** for the patient.
3. **Bill the patient** under your financial policy if no other coverage applies.

## Prevent it next time

- Check benefit limits during verification for services that commonly have them.
