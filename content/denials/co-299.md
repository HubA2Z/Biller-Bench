## What it means

X12 describes code 299 as **the billing provider is not eligible to receive payment for the service billed**.

## Common causes

1. **The billing group isn't enrolled** with the payer, or isn't enrolled for this service type.
2. **The wrong billing entity** (for example, a new group whose enrollment isn't approved).
3. **Reassignment** from the rendering provider to the group isn't on file.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the billing entity's enrollment** and effective date.
2. **Make sure each rendering provider's payments are reassigned** to the group where required.
3. **Resubmit** once enrollment is correct.

## Prevent it next time

- Finish group enrollment and reassignments before billing under a new entity.
