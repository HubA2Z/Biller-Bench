## What it means

X12 describes code 234 as **this procedure is not paid separately**, and it must come with at least one remark code. It's similar to CO-97, but used when the service is never separately payable under the payer's rules.

## Common causes

1. **Supplies or services** the payer always bundles into another service.
2. **Status-indicator rules** (for example, codes Medicare lists as bundled or packaged).
3. **Facility-packaged items** on outpatient claims.

## How to fix it

1. **Read the remark code** for the payer's reason.
2. **Check the payer's policy** or the fee schedule status for that code.
3. **If the code is never separately payable,** adjust it off.
4. **If it should be payable** (for example, billed in a different setting), correct the claim or appeal.

## Prevent it next time

- Flag always-bundled codes in your charge master.
- Keep payer bundling policies for your top codes.
