## What it means

X12 describes code 268 as **the claim spans two calendar years; please resubmit one claim per calendar year**. Deductibles, benefits and fee schedules reset by year, so payers need them separate.

## Common causes

1. **Monthly or span-dated services** that run from December into January.
2. **Therapy or DME** billed for a period crossing the year.

## How to fix it

1. **Split the claim** at December 31.
2. **Use the correct year's codes and fee schedule** on each claim.
3. **Resubmit both claims.**

## Prevent it next time

- End span-dated claims at December 31.
