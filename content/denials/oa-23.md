## What it means

X12 describes code 23 as **the impact of prior payer(s) adjudication including payments and/or adjustments**, used only with group code **OA**.

## Common causes

1. **Normal secondary processing:** the primary paid, so the secondary pays less or nothing.
2. **The secondary's allowed amount** is lower than what the primary already paid.

## How to fix it

1. **Compare with the primary remittance:** primary payment, patient responsibility and adjustments.
2. **If the numbers match,** post the adjustment.
3. **If they don't,** check that the secondary claim reported the primary's amounts and reason codes correctly, and correct the claim if needed.

## Prevent it next time

- Send the primary's 835 data with secondary claims electronically.
