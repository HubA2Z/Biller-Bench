## What it means

X12 describes code 24 as charges covered under a **capitation agreement or managed care plan**. Either the patient's care is paid through a monthly capitation payment to a provider group, or the patient belongs to a managed care plan that should be billed instead.

## Common causes

1. **The patient is enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan** and the claim was sent to Original Medicare.
2. **The patient is in a Medicaid managed care plan** and the claim went to fee-for-service Medicaid.
3. **Your practice is capitated** for this patient, so the service is included in the capitation payment.

## How to fix it

1. **Check eligibility for the date of service** to find the patient's managed care plan.
2. **If another plan is responsible,** bill that plan and watch its filing deadline.
3. **If your practice is capitated,** confirm the service is included under the capitation contract. Some services are carved out and billable separately.
4. **Submit an encounter** if your capitation contract requires it, even when there's no separate payment.

## Prevent it next time

- Verify eligibility at every visit; patients switch to Medicare Advantage or managed Medicaid often.
- Keep a list of carve-outs for each capitation contract.
