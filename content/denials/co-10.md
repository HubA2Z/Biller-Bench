## What it means

X12 describes code 10 as **the diagnosis is inconsistent with the patient's gender**. Some ICD-10-CM codes are edited as applying to one sex only.

## Common causes

1. **Wrong sex** in registration.
2. **The wrong patient** on the claim.
3. **A correct diagnosis** for a patient whose recorded sex doesn't match the anatomy involved.

## How to fix it

1. **Check registration and eligibility data.**
2. **Correct registration errors** and resubmit.
3. **If the code is clinically correct,** follow the payer's override instructions (Medicare uses condition code 45 on institutional claims and modifier KX on professional claims; commercial payers vary).

## Prevent it next time

- Verify demographics at registration.
- Keep payer-specific instructions for sex-edit overrides.
