## What it means

X12 describes code 21 as **this injury/illness is the liability of the no-fault carrier**. In no-fault states, auto insurance pays medical bills first regardless of who caused the accident.

## Common causes

1. **A car accident** with no-fault or PIP coverage.
2. **The accident indicator** on the claim marked "auto accident."

## How to fix it

1. **Get the auto carrier's details** and claim number.
2. **Bill the no-fault carrier** following your state's rules.
3. **When no-fault benefits are exhausted or denied,** send the explanation to the health plan with the claim.

## Prevent it next time

- Ask about auto accidents at intake.
- Keep a list of your state's no-fault billing rules and forms.
