## What it means

X12 describes code 39 as **services denied at the time authorization/pre-certification was requested**. The authorization was requested and refused.

## Common causes

1. **The service went ahead** after the authorization was denied.
2. **A peer-to-peer review or appeal** of the authorization wasn't completed.
3. **The claim was linked to a denied request** instead of a later approved one.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the authorization history** on the payer portal.
2. **If a later request was approved,** put that authorization number on the claim and resubmit.
3. **If not,** appeal the denial with clinical documentation, following the plan's appeal rules and deadlines.
4. **If the patient was told in advance** and agreed in writing to pay (where the plan and state rules allow), bill per that agreement; otherwise adjust it off.

## Prevent it next time

- Don't schedule services until authorization is approved, unless the case is urgent.
- Use peer-to-peer reviews quickly after an authorization denial.
