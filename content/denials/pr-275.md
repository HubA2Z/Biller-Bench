## What it means

X12 describes code 275 as **prior payer's (or payers') patient responsibility (deductible, coinsurance, co-payment) not covered**, used only with group code **PR**.

## Common causes

1. **The secondary plan doesn't cover** the primary's cost sharing (for example, a plan that only pays up to its own allowed amount).
2. **The secondary's own deductible** wasn't met.

## How to fix it

1. **Confirm the secondary plan's COB method** and benefits.
2. **Bill the patient** for the remaining amount under your financial policy.
3. **Check protections first:** some patients can't be billed for cost sharing, such as Medicare patients in the Qualified Medicare Beneficiary (QMB) program, or Medicaid patients in many cases.

## Prevent it next time

- Verify secondary benefits, not just secondary coverage.
- Flag QMB and dual-eligible patients in your system.
