## What it means

X12 describes code 151 as **payment adjusted because the payer deems the information submitted does not support this many/frequency of services**.

## Common causes

1. **Units above the payer's daily limit.** For Medicare, these are Medically Unlikely Edits (MUEs).
2. **A test or service repeated** sooner than the policy allows.
3. **Unit errors,** such as minutes entered as units.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the units** against the documentation and code definition.
2. **Check the payer's limit** (MUE value for Medicare).
3. **If the units were wrong,** correct the claim.
4. **If more units were medically necessary,** appeal with records explaining why.

## Prevent it next time

- Add MUE and frequency checks to your claim scrubber.
- Use the correct unit definitions for timed codes and drugs.
