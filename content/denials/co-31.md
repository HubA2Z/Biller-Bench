## What it means

X12 describes code 31 as **the patient cannot be identified as our insured**. The payer couldn't match the member ID, name and birth date on the claim to anyone in its records.

## Common causes

1. **Member ID typo,** or a missing prefix or suffix.
2. **Name mismatch,** such as a nickname, a hyphenated or changed surname, or the subscriber's name used for a dependent.
3. **Wrong birth date** in registration.
4. **Wrong payer,** for example a plan administered by a different company than the card's logo suggests.

## How to fix it

1. **Run a fresh eligibility check** with the card details and compare every field with your registration.
2. **Correct the patient record** and resubmit the claim.
3. **For dependents,** make sure the subscriber and patient are listed correctly and the relationship code is right.
4. **If the card lists a separate claims address or payer ID,** send the claim there.

## Prevent it next time

- Scan both sides of the card at every visit.
- Copy the member ID from the eligibility response, not by hand.
- Match names exactly as the payer has them.
