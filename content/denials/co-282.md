## What it means

X12 describes code 282 as **the procedure/revenue code is inconsistent with the type of bill**. The type of bill (TOB) on a UB-04 / 837I claim tells the payer the facility type and setting.

## Common causes

1. **Wrong type of bill,** such as outpatient services billed on an inpatient TOB.
2. **Codes not valid for that setting** under the payer's rules.
3. **Charge master mapping** errors.

## How to fix it

1. **Confirm the patient's status and setting** for the dates of service.
2. **Correct the type of bill or the code** and resubmit.

## Prevent it next time

- Review type-of-bill logic when patient status changes (for example, observation to inpatient).
- Add TOB and revenue code edits to your claim scrubber.
