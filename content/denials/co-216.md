## What it means

X12 describes code 216 as **based on the findings of a review organization**.

## Common causes

1. **A medical review or audit** found the documentation didn't support the service.
2. **Utilization review** decisions for facility stays.

## How to fix it

1. **Get the review findings** and the specific reason.
2. **Gather the documentation** that addresses each finding.
3. **Appeal within the deadline** stated in the review letter.

## Prevent it next time

- Audit documentation for services that payers often review.
