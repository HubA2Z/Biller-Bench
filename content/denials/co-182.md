## What it means

X12 describes code 182 as **procedure modifier was invalid on the date of service**.

## Common causes

1. **A retired or replaced modifier** still in a template.
2. **A new modifier** used before the payer recognizes it.
3. **A typo** (for example, a letter O instead of a zero).

## How to fix it

1. **Check the modifier's validity** for the date of service and the payer.
2. **Replace it** with the correct modifier and resubmit.

## Prevent it next time

- Review modifier lists in your templates after each code update.
- Add modifier validity edits in your claim scrubber.
