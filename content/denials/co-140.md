## What it means

X12 describes code 140 as **patient/insured health identification number and name do not match**. The payer found the ID but the name on the claim isn't the name it has for that ID.

## Common causes

1. **Nickname or spelling difference** (Bill vs. William, missing hyphen).
2. **Name change** after marriage or divorce that the payer hasn't processed, or that your system hasn't.
3. **Dependent billed with the subscriber's name,** or the reverse.
4. **Suffix or middle name** placed in the wrong field.

## How to fix it

1. **Run an eligibility check** and copy the name exactly as the payer returns it.
2. **Check subscriber vs. patient fields** and the relationship code.
3. **Correct the claim and resubmit.**
4. **If the patient's legal name changed,** ask them to update it with the plan as well.

## Prevent it next time

- Register patients with the name exactly as it appears on the insurance card.
- Use eligibility responses to auto-fill names and IDs where your system allows.
