## What it means

X12 describes code 287 as **referral exceeded**.

## Common causes

1. **More visits** than the referral approved.
2. **Visits after the referral's end date.**
3. **Services outside the referral's scope.**

## How to fix it

1. **Check the referral's limits** (visits, dates and services).
2. **Request an extended or new referral** from the PCP; some plans allow it retroactively.
3. **Resubmit with the updated referral.**

## Prevent it next time

- Track remaining referral visits at every check-in.
- Request renewals before the last approved visit.
