## What it means

X12 describes code 183 as **the referring provider is not eligible to refer the service billed**.

## Common causes

1. **The referring provider isn't enrolled** with the payer (for Medicare, not enrolled or not approved in PECOS).
2. **A provider type** that isn't allowed to refer that service.
3. **Wrong NPI or name** for the referring provider on the claim.
4. **A group NPI** used where an individual NPI is required.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the referring provider's NPI** and name against the order or referral.
2. **Check enrollment:** for Medicare, look the provider up in the CMS ordering and referring data.
3. **Correct the claim** with the right individual NPI, or ask the referring provider to enroll, then resubmit.

## Prevent it next time

- Verify ordering and referring providers before the service, especially for labs, imaging, DME and therapy.
