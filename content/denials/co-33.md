## What it means

X12 describes code 33 as **insured has no dependent coverage**. The policy covers the subscriber only (an employee-only plan, for example), so no dependents are covered under it.

## Common causes

1. **Employee-only enrollment,** even though family members use the same card information.
2. **The claim was billed under the wrong subscriber.**
3. **Dependent coverage ended** at a plan change or open enrollment.

## How to fix it

1. **Confirm with the patient** what coverage they actually have.
2. **Check whether the patient has their own policy** or is covered under another family member's plan.
3. **Bill the correct plan,** or the patient under your financial policy if there's no coverage.

## Prevent it next time

- Verify the patient, not just the subscriber, at every visit.
- Ask for the patient's own card when they're not the subscriber.
