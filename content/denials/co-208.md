## What it means

X12 describes code 208 as **National Provider Identifier - not matched**.

## Common causes

1. **The NPI isn't linked** to the billing tax ID or group with the payer.
2. **A new location or address** not on file with the payer.
3. **Individual and group NPIs swapped** on the claim.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the payer's enrollment record** for the provider: NPI, tax ID, group and locations.
2. **Correct the claim** if the NPIs were in the wrong fields.
3. **Update enrollment** if the payer's record is wrong, then resubmit.

## Prevent it next time

- Update payers when providers join, leave or move locations.
