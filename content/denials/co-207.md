## What it means

X12 describes code 207 as **National Provider Identifier - invalid format**.

## Common causes

1. **Typos** or missing digits.
2. **Another number in the NPI field,** such as a tax ID or legacy payer number.

## How to fix it

1. **Look up the correct NPI** in the NPPES NPI Registry.
2. **Correct it** in your billing system and on the claim.
3. **Resubmit.**

## Prevent it next time

- Validate NPIs with the check digit when you add providers.
