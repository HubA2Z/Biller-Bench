## What it means

X12 describes code 34 as **insured has no coverage for newborns**. The payer can't pay for the newborn's services under this policy.

## Common causes

1. **The baby hasn't been enrolled** on a parent's plan yet.
2. **The claim was billed under the mother's member ID** when the plan needs the baby's own ID.
3. **The parent's plan doesn't offer dependent coverage.**
4. **Medicaid newborn coverage** is pending.

## How to fix it

1. **Ask the family** whether the baby has been added and to which plan.
2. **Remind them of the enrollment deadline;** many plans allow a limited window after birth.
3. **Once enrolled,** bill with the baby's own member ID and details.
4. **If the family qualifies for Medicaid,** check newborn eligibility in your state's system.

## Prevent it next time

- At the first newborn visit, collect the planned coverage and remind parents to enroll.
- Hold newborn claims briefly until the baby's coverage shows on eligibility.
